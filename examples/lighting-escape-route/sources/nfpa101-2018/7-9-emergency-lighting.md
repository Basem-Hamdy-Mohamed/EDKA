- ⟦p.314⟧ **7.9.2.1** Duration **1½ hours**.
- ⟦p.314⟧ **7.9.2.1.1** Initial average at least 1 ft-candle (**10.8 lux**); any point at least
  0.1 ft-candle (**1.1 lux**).
- ⟦p.314⟧ **7.9.2.1.2** At the end of 1½ hours: average at least 0.6 ft-candle (**6.5 lux**) and any point
  0.06 ft-candle (**0.65 lux**).
- ⟦p.314⟧ **7.9.2.1.3** Maximum-to-minimum ratio not above 40 to 1.
