Paraphrase. This is the **1999** edition, a UK deviation from BS EN 1838.

- ⟦p.7⟧ **5.3.2** Centre line of the escape route: not less than **0.2 lx**, preferably **1 lx**;
  at points of emphasis not less than **1 lx**.
- ⟦p.8⟧ **5.3.2** For routes up to 2 m wide, **50 %** of the route width lit to at least **0.1 lx**.
