- ⟦p.229⟧ **1008.2.1** Normal power: egress illumination at the walking surface at least **11 lux**.
- ⟦p.230⟧ **1008.3.4** Emergency power for egress illumination: duration at least **90 minutes**
  (storage batteries, unit equipment or an on-site generator).
- ⟦p.230⟧ **1008.3.5** On emergency power: initial average at least **11 lux** and minimum at any point
  **1 lux** along the path of egress at floor level; may decline to **6 lux** average and **0.6 lux**
  minimum at the end of the duration; maximum-to-minimum ratio not above 40 to 1.
