- ⟦p.15⟧ **3.4.2** Design value at least **1 lx** on the floor along the centre line, **0.5 lx** on the
  centre band of at least **50%** of the route width; centre-line uniformity better than 40:1.
- ⟦p.41⟧ **7** Duration is now normally **3 hours**, and always for sleeping risk, cinemas, theatres and
  licensed premises. One hour only where the building is evacuated at once and not reoccupied until
  the batteries are recharged.
