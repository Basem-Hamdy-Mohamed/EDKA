- ⟦p.6⟧ **4.2.1** Routes up to 2 m wide: at least **1 lx** on the centre line, and a central band of at
  least half the route width lit to at least **50 %** of that value.
- ⟦p.7⟧ **4.2.2** Ratio of maximum to minimum along the centre line not greater than 40:1.
- ⟦p.7⟧ **4.2.5** Minimum duration for escape purposes **1 h**.
