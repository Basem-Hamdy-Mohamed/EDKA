DEC 2024 sets one total limit of 4 % from the origin of the installation to the equipment. It does not cover motor starting.

⟦p.3⟧
> 4.1 General. Voltage drop shall be calculated at the design current of each circuit.
>
> 4.2 Limits. The total voltage drop from the origin of the installation to any current-using equipment shall not exceed 4 %.

⟦p.4⟧

| Part of installation | Lighting | Other uses |
|---|---|---|
| Origin to equipment (total) | 4 % | 4 % |

> 4.3 Motor starting is not covered by 4.2.
